## Why Archive?

These documents served their purpose during active development phases but are no longer part of the current documentation set. They're preserved here for:

1. **Historical Reference** - Understanding past decisions and evolution
2. **Audit Trail** - Tracking quality improvements over time
3. **Learning** - Reviewing what worked and what didn't
4. **Continuity** - Helping future contributors understand the project's journey
